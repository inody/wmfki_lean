Primitive inputs

An initial probability measure π, a Brownian base measure P, a measurable L⁴ initial variable ξ, an independent Brownian family W, a Lipschitz interaction kernel K, test functions φ, ψ, and dictionary atoms.

Not additionally assumed: Gaussian initial data, independence of initial coordinates, K(0)=0, boundedness of the true kernel, or independence of the interacting particles.

1

Construct the mean-field process

Picard iteration produces a finite-horizon McKean–Vlasov process Y. Lean proves uniqueness within the stated finite-L⁴ path class, adaptedness, and consistency across finite horizons.

MeanFieldPicardLimit.lean · MeanFieldWellPosedness.lean
2

Place independent copies and interacting particles on shared drivers

Lean constructs iid copies Y₁,…,Y_N and an interacting particle system Xᴺ driven by the same initial and Brownian arrays. Independence of the copies is never transferred incorrectly to the interacting particles.

NonlinearCopiesFromInputs.lean · SynchronousArrayEquation.lean
3

Control the synchronous error in fourth moment

The proof handles common-tag fluctuations conditionally, preserves the off-diagonal exclusion, and then applies a Gronwall comparison. The constant is chosen before quantifying over N.

E ‖Xᵢᴺ(t) − Yᵢ(t)‖⁴ ≤ C / N²
SynchronousFourthRate.lean
4

Prove the design and residual bounds separately

Design branch D₀

Feature differences use the actual gradient ∇φ. They are bounded by Cauchy–Schwarz with dependent L⁴ factors, not by an independence argument. Measurability, L² integrability, and Fubini conditions for the actual time kernel are proved before connecting the iid-pair estimate to the μₜ×μₜ population design.

E ‖Aᴺ_cts − A_pop‖F² ≤ C M / N

Residual branch V₀

The continuous weak residual is constructed from the particle SDE, the Itô weak identity, Brownian covariance and isometry, and integration by parts in time. It is not replaced by an abstract residual input.

continuous weak residual energy ∝ σ² / N
ActualDesignCoupling.lean · ContinuousPopulationRate.lean · EmpiricalResidualEnergy.lean
5

Add observation and quadrature errors on the same realization

Observation noise is required to be independent only of the latent system. It may be biased and dependent across particles, times, and coordinates; no fourth-moment assumption on the observations is imposed.

D₀ ≤ C_A (1/N + e + q)
V₀ ≤ C_V (σ²/N + e + q)

One may read e = Δt²ᵖ and q = Cε σobs². The RMS quadrature accuracy itself remains an explicit assumption, shown in yellow.

Identify the paper expressions with production definitions

Exact bridge lemmas rewrite the new continuous and discrete designs as fullPairDesign, the derivative-based response as observedWeakResponse, and their difference as fullRegressionResidual.

Add random-pair sampling and invoke both estimators

Uniform directed off-diagonal pairs are sampled with replacement, with one sampling sequence shared by all rows and dictionary atoms. Conditional centering contributes the Q/P term, after which the existing OLS and LASSO recovery APIs apply.

OLS: ‖θ̂−θ*‖² ≤ 4 V /(ε γ²)
LASSO: ‖θ̂−θ*‖² ≤ 144 |S| λ²/γ⁴

The important quantifier order

The proof does not choose a convenient constant separately for each N. The model and common constants are fixed first; only then does the theorem quantify over all particle counts.

First, common objects exist

∃ Y, R, Rpos, CA, CV

The mean-field solution and common moment and error constants.

Then, for every particle count

∀ N, 0 < N → ∃ BN, X

A particle system coupled to the same Y and common constants.

Finally, for every observation design

∀ grid, noise, e, q

The same D₀/V₀ form under the stated L² and quadrature conditions.

What this map does not claim

Outside the present scope

The formalization does not establish universal transfer to an arbitrary pre-existing joint realization, additional adaptedness of the copies to a newly constructed particle filtration, a uniform-in-time rate, measurability of the estimators themselves, correctness of a numerical solver, or support recovery.

What about N=0 and degenerate cases?

The rates are asserted only for N>0. Definitions and supporting lemmas separately inspect N=0,1, T=0, and zero-energy cases rather than hiding a division by zero.

Does “zero project axioms” mean that everything was proved?

No. It means that the declared theorems do not depend on project-specific placeholder axioms. The RMS quadrature, geometric, and penalty conditions shown in yellow remain explicit hypotheses of the theorem.
